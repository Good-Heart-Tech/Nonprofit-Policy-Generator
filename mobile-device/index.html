A Mobile Device Policy sets security and usage requirements for phones and tablets used to access your organization's data, whether the device is organization-owned or personal (BYOD).

A lost phone with access to your email or donor database is one of the most common ways nonprofits lose sensitive data. Requiring a passcode and remote wipe consent are two of the cheapest ways to reduce that risk.
